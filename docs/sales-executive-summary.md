---

## Product

**Mousumi ERP** is an integrated Human Resource, Payroll and Operations platform for multi-branch organizations in Bangladesh. One login. One employee master. One workflow from joining to final settlement.

It is not an attendance-only tool. Live modules cover HR, biometric + mobile attendance, field movement, leave, payroll, staff loans, provident fund, gratuity, fixed assets and inventory.

**Live reference:** running at **MOUSUMI**, Ukilpara, Naogaon. White-label ready (your name, logo, report headers, tax slabs, leave rules, organogram).

## Live modules (10 + map)

1. **Human Resources** — 360° employee file, org chart, confirmation, promotion, demotion, transfer (incl. bulk), disciplinary, separation, documents, Excel import
2. **Attendance & Movement** — ZKTeco / iClock ADMS, GPS geofence punch, device lock, monthly grid, field movement + logbook payment + penalty lock
3. **Leave** — types, balances, multi-tier approval, PDF, bulk year allocation
4. **Payroll** — payscale → grade → step, process/post/rollback, bonus run, tax slabs, bank advice, 17 reports, payslips
5. **Employee Loan** — policy → committee → disburse → payroll/manual collection, migration, 10 reports
6. **Staff Fund** — PF ledger & interest, gratuity liability, final payment, financial statement
7. **Fixed Asset** — purchase to disposal, Jul–Jun FY depreciation, audit schedules, 11 reports
8. **Inventory** — stock in, issue to staff, ledgers
9. **Administration** — users, 15+ roles, section locks, session revoke, notices + Web Push
10. **Office Map** — branches / zones on a map

**Self-service:** punch, leave, movement, payslip, PF/loan ledger, my assets — on phone (PWA).

---

## Built for Bangladesh

Grade–step salary · income-tax slabs · PF 50/50 interest split · tenure-based gratuity · Friday–Saturday weekends (per-branch override) · microfinance organogram (ED → ZM → RM → BM) · Taka format · report signature blocks (HR / F&A / ED)

---

## Who uses it

Super Admin, HR, Accounts, ED/Directors, Zonal/Regional/Branch Managers, Department Heads, PIN-only **branch terminal**, and employees. Data is scoped by posting — a BM sees own branch, not the whole country.

---

## Honest scope (do not oversell)

**Not in this release:** Recruitment ATS, Training LMS, rotating shift roster, SMS gateway, multi-company SaaS, full store indent module.

**Fit:** one legal entity, many branches. Group of companies = separate install or future phase.
